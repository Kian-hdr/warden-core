# Model weights and access requests

Warden Core model weights, checkpoints, optimizer state, embeddings and exported policy binaries are **closed source**. No model-weight files are distributed in this repository.

The public repository provides the engineering architecture, reward-policy mathematics, experiment methodology, selected verified results, runnable observability software and reviewed media. Keeping model artifacts outside the public repository preserves their provenance, usage controls and commercial-licensing path.

## Commercial access

Organizations interested in commercial evaluation, integration or deployment may request access under a separate written paid agreement. A request should identify:

- the organization and intended product or service;
- the requested model or capability;
- the intended hardware, runtime and field of use;
- evaluation, modification and distribution requirements;
- security and model-handling controls; and
- the requested evaluation and commercial timeline.

Commercial access begins only after the applicable rights holder and licensee sign an agreement defining the permitted model, use, term, security, support and fees. See [commercial licensing](commercial-licensing.md).

## Education and academic research

Students, educators and academic researchers may request access for a defined noncommercial teaching or research project. Include the institution, supervisor or course where applicable, research purpose, requested artifact, handling plan and intended publication or demonstration.

Education or academic access is reviewed case by case and may require a research-use agreement, attribution, non-redistribution terms and responsible model handling. A request does not guarantee access, and the project may provide documentation, a bounded evaluation artifact or supervised access instead of transferable weights.
