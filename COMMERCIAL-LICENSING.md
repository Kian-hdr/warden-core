# Commercial licensing

The public licences in this repository permit the stated noncommercial uses. They do not grant commercial rights to the project-owned software, documentation, media or hardware designs.

Commercial licences may be available through a separate written paid agreement with the applicable rights holder. A commercial agreement can define the exact files, field of use, organization, term, permitted modifications, distribution, support, warranties and price.

To discuss a licence, contact [Kian Tajbakhsh through the repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kian-hdr) and include:

- your organization and contact details;
- the files or components you want to use;
- the product, service, research programme or internal use involved;
- whether you plan to modify, fabricate, distribute or sublicense anything;
- the intended users, territory and expected duration; and
- your desired evaluation and production timeline.

An inquiry, discussion, quotation or access to the public repository does not grant commercial rights. Commercial use begins only after the applicable rights holder and the licensee sign a written agreement covering that use.

Third-party and teammate-owned material is outside this offer unless its rights holder separately participates in the agreement.
